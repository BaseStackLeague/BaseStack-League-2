## Registration and Seeding

- **VRS filter:** Global
- **Rank:** Tier 2
- **Invitations:** No direct VRS invitations. Registration is open to all teams.
- **Registration order:** The 12 highest VRS-ranked teams from the Open Registration will be selected first in the Team Selection and contacted on November 13. After that, selection will continue down based on the VRS ranking, starting with Team #13, followed by Team #14, #15, and so on. If it comes down to teams with no VRS rank, selection will be based on their registration timestamp.
To be included in the initial Team Selection from the Open Registration, starting on November 13th, teams must have completed their registration by November 12 at 23:59 CET at the latest. Registration will remain open after the initial Team Selection. If slots remain available, additional teams may still register and be considered for participation.
Registration fully closes on November 15 at 23:59 CET, 2026. After this deadline, no new teams can be accepted to participate
- **Seeding:** Based on the VRS ranking published on 2nd November 2026.
- 1️⃣ VRS ranking (November 2nd, 2026) 
- 2️⃣ HLTV ranking (November 2nd, 2026)
- 3️⃣ Avg FACEIT ELO (5 players) 
- Registration at (https://forms.gle/cBxpQ9rM43VYpQ8s6) and entry fee payment 2000PLN(+23% VAT)
- No sibling or academy teams from same org


## Tournament Format

The format depends on the number of participating teams. The playoffs will feature **8 teams** from the main stage.

| Teams | Format | Days |
|------:|--------|:----:|
| 8 | 8-team double-elimination bracket, played through to the Grand Final | 2 |
| 9 | Play-in: seed 8 vs seed 9 (BO3) for the last spot, followed by the 8-team double-elimination main event | 3 |
| 10 | Group Stage: one 4-team double-elimination (GSL) group with seeds 7–10, where the top 2 join seeds 1–6 in the main event; followed by the 8-team double-elimination main event | 3 |
| 11 | Group Stage: two groups with seeds 5–11 (one 4-team double-elimination group, one 3-team group double-elimination group), where the top 2 from each join seeds 1–4 in the main event; followed by the 8-team double-elimination main event | 3 |
| 12 | Group Stage: two 4-team double-elimination (GSL) groups with seeds 5–12, where the top 2 from each join seeds 1–4 in the main event; followed by the 8-team double-elimination main event | 3 |

## VRS Status

The event is intended to meet Valve's Ranked Tier 2 requirements for tournaments without direct VRS invitations. Following our application, HLTV.org will determine which stages and matches count towards the VRS.

**VRS ranking status is not guaranteed to be confirmed before the event.** We are in direct contact with HLTV.org and will apply at the recommended time.

## Travel Requirements

All players must be able to enter Poland and participate in the LAN tournament. Teams are responsible for obtaining any required passports and visas.

The tournament operator may request proof that a team can attend the event. A team that cannot enter Poland may be excluded from any stage of the tournament.
