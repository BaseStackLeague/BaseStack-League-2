## 4.1. Game version
The latest available version of the game will be used during the tournament. In the 
event that the latest version is considered unplayable, the tournament 
administration may decide to play the tournament on the latest playable version of 
the client (if technically possible).

- Bomb timer: **40s** (`mp_c4timer 40`)  
- Overtime rounds: **Best out of 6** (`mp_overtime_maxrounds 6`)  
- Overtime start money: **$10 000** (`mp_overtime_startmoney 10000`)  
- Round restart delay: **5s** (`mp_round_restart_delay 5`)  
- Break during halftime in OT: **disabled**

## 4.8. Tactical pause
A team can use the pause option in the game menu (ESC -> Call Vote -> Call 
Tactical Timeout) to order a tactical pause. During regulation game time, each team 
has 3 tactical pauses, each lasting 30 seconds. 
 
During overtime, each team has only 1 tactical pause of 30 seconds each for each 
overtime period. 

## 4.9. Technical pause
In the event of a technical problem preventing the match from being played, a team 
may use the “.tech” command to pause the game. The pause will start during the 
next freezetime. It is the team's responsibility to immediately inform the 
administrator of the reason for using a technical pause.

## 4.10. Agent skins
Agent skins are **not allowed.**  

## 4.11. Higher seed
The starting position of a team during a Map Veto is determined by the following rule: coin toss

## 4.12. BO1 veto
The winner of toss a coin team decides whether it wants to be the A or B team. Team A 
begins the veto procedure with the following order:
  1. Team A removes 1 map  
  2. Team B removes 1 map 
  3. Team A removes 1 map  
  4. Team B removes 1 map 
  5. Team A removes 1 map  
  6. Team B removes 1 map 
  7. Remaining map is played  
- Starting sides: knife round  

## 4.13. BO3 veto
BO3 veto 
The winner of toss a coin team decides whether it wants to be the A or B team. Team A 
begins the veto procedure with the following order: 
  1. Team A removes 1 map  
  2. Team B removes 1 map  
  3. Team A picks 1 map (Team B chooses side)  
  4. Team B picks 1 map (Team A chooses side)  
  5. Team A removes 1 map  
  6. Team B removes 1 map  
  7. Remaining map is decider (knife round decides sides)  

## 4.14. Veto time
Veto time 
Map veto starts 45 minutes before the scheduled start time of the match. Teams 
have 30 minutes to conduct map veto. If one of the teams does not conduct the 
veto within the above mentioned time, they may receive a walkover penalty. 

## 4.15. Coaches
During LAN matches, the coach may be present behind the players during the 
game. The coach will be connected to the same voice channel as his team, but can 
only communicate during tactical pauses and halftimes.

## 4.16. Match rescheduling
In the event of an exceptional emergency, the operator may consider postponing a 
match. However, such a decision will only be made after careful consideration of the 
circumstances. A match may only be postponed with the mutual consent of all 
teams directly affected by the postponement.

# SECTION VI - Integrity and conduct

## 6.1. Cheating
The use of any cheating software/hardware is prohibited. A player who uses such 
aids may be disqualified from the competition. 

## 6.2. Betting
Players, staff and the operator must not be  involved in betting on any matches in 
the competition. 

## 6.3. Use of third-party information
The use of information obtained by fraudulent means (e.g. third-party assistance, 
stream sniping, etc.) during the tournament is prohibited. 

## 6.4. Match-fixing
Match fixing during the tournament is strictly prohibited. 

## 6.5. Bugs & glitches
The use of bugs, glitches that can give a disproportionate advantage over 
opponents is prohibited. Exceptions are bugs commonly used for part of gameplay. 
In case of doubt, a team may consult the admissibility of a bug with an 
administrator.  

## 6.6. Penalties
The final form of punishment for the above offenses is determined by the 
administrator on a case-by-case basis.
